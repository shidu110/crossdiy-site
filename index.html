NX Product & Mold Design

Product & Mold Design

Surfacing and structural design for automotive interior/exterior trim and consumer products — the full Siemens NX workflow: concept surfaces, engineering drawings, mold parting and manufacturability assessment.

  • SendSketches, references, existing CAD or a physical sample.
  • DeliverablesNative NX .prt, STEP AP242, 2D drawings, parting & DFM notes.

/ 02

Reverse Engineering

Reverse Engineering

Rebuilding 3D scan data (STL meshes) into mold-ready STEP solids. Outer surfaces are built to CAS standard with G2 continuity, and every delivery includes a deviation inspection report.

  • SendSTL/PLY scan data, the part's intended use, and any must-keep dimensions.
  • DeliverablesMold-ready STEP AP242 solid plus a deviation inspection report.
  • OutcomeCAS-grade outer surfaces with G2 continuity, ready for downstream mold design.

Complete small-product development — concept, surfacing, structure, prototype. Alongside my own product line (ergonomic split keyboards), I take on small-batch product development commissions.

  • SendA short brief: the idea, constraints, target quantity and cost range if known.
  • DeliverablesConcept surfaces, structure, prototype-ready CAD and iteration support.
  • OutcomeOne accountable path from first sketch to a physical prototype.

QuestionsFAQ

What should I send for a quote?

Your part files (STEP, STL or scan data), drawings or photos, the material, target quantity and deadline. If the product is unreleased, ask for an NDA first.

What exactly will I receive?

Whatever the written scope lists — typically native NX .prt files, STEP AP242 / Parasolid-ready neutral files, 2D drawings, and for reverse-engineering work a deviation inspection report.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. I am happy to work under an NDA, and project files are treated as confidential.

Who owns the design and the IP?

Ownership and usage terms are agreed in writing in the scope, before work starts — no surprises afterwards.

How are revisions handled?

The number of included revision rounds is stated in the scope. Anything beyond that, or a change of requirements, is quoted before the extra work begins.

How is the price set?

A fixed quote after I have reviewed your files — scope, deliverables and date are confirmed in writing. Ongoing support can be booked by the day instead.

Can you work directly with my mold shop?

Yes. I can exchange files and DFM comments directly with your manufacturer, in English or Chinese.
