AboutProfile

Mold design is my formal training, and making things real is what I have kept doing ever since — from surfaces in NX, to reverse-engineered solids from scans, to keyboards and games.

Programming and product development are self-taught, which is why I trust measurement over intuition: strategies get backtested, surfaces get deviation-checked, conclusions need data.

This site is my own digital front door. On social platforms an algorithm decides who sees what; here, I decide.
